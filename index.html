Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 29 July 2026

Sudoku Journey ("the App") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect your information.

Information We Collect

The App does not require registration and does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Identity information

The App stores only local game information on your device, including:

  • Saved Sudoku progress
  • Best scores
  • Language settings
  • Sound settings

This information never leaves your device.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google may collect anonymous information including:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device information
  • Approximate location
  • Crash reports
  • Performance information

Advertising data is handled by Google according to Google's Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information

  • Display advertisements
  • Improve app stability
  • Fix bugs
  • Improve game performance

Third-Party Services

This App uses:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user information.
